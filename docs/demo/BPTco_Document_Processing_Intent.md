# BPTco — Document Processing and Enrichment Intent

## Business process to improve

Use AI in BPTco to streamline the processing and enrichment of business documents, such as invoices, contracts, and supporting attachments. Automatically classify documents, extract key information—including names, reference numbers, dates, and amounts—and enrich them with standardized metadata and concise summaries. Validate extracted information against business rules and route exceptions or low-confidence results for human review.

## Expected business outcome

Reduce manual data-entry effort by 60% and document-processing time by 50%, while targeting 95% accuracy for critical fields across supported document types. Improve document searchability, data consistency, and downstream reporting so employees can spend less time reviewing documents and more time resolving exceptions.

## Required boundaries

- Maintain controlled, least-privilege access to source documents and extracted information.
- Require human approval before critical records or downstream systems are updated.
- Preserve source references, confidence, exceptions, and an auditable processing history.
- Keep the existing workflow available when the AI capability is unavailable or uncertain.
- Treat all outcome targets and usage volumes as customer-provided assumptions until measured in a bounded proof of concept.
