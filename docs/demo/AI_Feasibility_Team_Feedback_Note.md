# Team feedback note

## Suggested subject

Hackathon concept: Azure Migrate AI Feasibility Assessment — feedback welcome

## Message

Hi team,

During this hackathon, I explored a product concept for extending the Azure Migrate journey into AI modernization. Here is a short product-ideation demo of the working simulator prototype.

Azure Migrate already helps customers understand **where and how an application should run in Azure**. This concept adds a second decision:

> **Where can AI create meaningful value in an existing application, and what would it take to deliver that value responsibly?**

The proposed experience combines two inputs:

- **Existing application intelligence from Azure Migrate** — application boundaries, dependencies, runtimes, assessed Azure targets, readiness, cost, and the current Cloud Topology Diagram (CTD) architecture.
- **Customer-provided AI intent** — the business workflow to improve or automate, users, desired outcome, data context, constraints, compliance needs, and expected usage.

Together, these inputs produce an **AI Feasibility Assessment**: a reviewable decision pack covering business fit, data and integration readiness, security and governance, delivery and operations, economics, evidence gaps, proposed Azure AI capabilities, indicative cost scenarios, and a bounded proof-of-concept plan.

The experience then carries the assessment into architecture. It preserves the existing CTD-based migration design and creates a linked **AI-extended architecture** that clearly shows the proposed AI services, data flows, controls, and incremental planning cost without changing or obscuring the baseline application architecture.

The goal is not to recommend AI for every application. The assessment may recommend a proof of concept, remediation first, conventional automation, an existing product, or no AI. The intent is to turn application context plus customer business intent into an explainable modernization decision and a practical handoff to Microsoft Foundry, field teams, or delivery partners.

This is an early product concept, and I would appreciate your thoughts on this ideation.

**Short demo:** Attach `Innovation_Studio_AI_Feasibility_Demo.mp4` (1 minute 53 seconds).

Thanks — feedback, challenges, and alternative ideas are very welcome.
